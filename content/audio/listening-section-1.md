## Script

**Narrator:** You will hear a conversation between a woman who wants to join a community centre and a receptionist. First you have some time to look at questions 1 to 6.

**Narrator:** Now listen carefully and answer questions 1 to 6.

**Receptionist:** Good morning, Riverside Community Centre. How can I help you?

**Caller:** Hello. I'm thinking of joining the centre, and I'd like to ask about the pottery class. Is it still on Wednesday evenings?

**Receptionist:** It used to be on Wednesdays, but it moved last term. A lot of people asked for a weekend class, so now it's on Saturday mornings, from ten until twelve.

**Caller:** Saturday mornings. That's actually better for me. And there's nothing on Monday?

**Receptionist:** No, Monday is our painting group. Pottery is only on Saturdays now.

**Caller:** Lovely. So what do I need to bring to my first session?

**Receptionist:** Two things. First, proof of address. A recent bank statement or a utility bill is fine. And second, the membership form, filled in and signed.

**Caller:** Do I need a passport photo as well?

**Receptionist:** No, you don't need a photo any more. We take your picture here when we print your card.

**Caller:** And is there a deposit to pay?

**Receptionist:** No, there's no deposit. And you don't need a medical certificate either. That's only for the swimming club.

**Caller:** Great. How much is the membership fee?

**Receptionist:** It's twelve pounds a month for students. Are you a student?

**Caller:** No, I'm not, I'm afraid.

**Receptionist:** Then it's fifteen pounds per month for adult members.

**Caller:** Fifteen pounds a month. That's reasonable. Is the centre open every day?

**Receptionist:** Almost. We're open seven days a week, but we close on the first Monday of every month, for cleaning and repairs.

**Caller:** The first Monday. I'll remember that. I'm also interested in yoga. Where is that class held?

**Receptionist:** Yoga used to be in the Main Hall, but it was too noisy in there, so the class has moved to the Garden Hall, at the back of the building.

**Caller:** The Garden Hall. And finally, when can I come in to enrol? I work during the week.

**Receptionist:** That's no problem. The enrolment desk is open at weekends as well, on Saturdays and Sundays, from nine until one.

**Caller:** Perfect. I'll come in on Saturday, then. Thank you very much.

**Receptionist:** You're welcome. Goodbye.

**Narrator:** That is the end of the conversation.
