## Design Context

### Users
Zambian secondary-school students (13-18, O-Level syllabus) and IELTS
candidates. Mostly on phones, on slow or expensive mobile data, reading
English as a second language, often outdoors. They want to understand one
topic, check they understood it, and keep the lesson for when there is no
internet. The current demo audience is Cosmic Computer Supplies (a Zambian IT
firm) evaluating the product as a possible partner.

### Brand Personality
Plain-spoken, exact, encouraging. Like a good teacher with a clean
chalkboard and a well-printed workbook. Confident, never childish, never
corporate. The product is completely free.

### Aesthetic Direction
"Workbook": paper-white pages, ink text, ruled lines and dotted leaders,
large Clarendon numerals (Besley), a plain-zero grotesque for body text
(Hanken Grotesk), chalkboard green as the brand colour and the High School
course, Zambian copper as the IELTS course and the one warm accent,
highlighter yellow for emphasis. One dark surface only: the chalkboard,
with faint chalk sketches. Bespoke two-colour illustrations instead of icon
tiles. Light theme (daytime phone use, bright surroundings).

Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboards (Inter + blue-600 + shadowed
rounded cards), gradient heroes, glassmorphism, pill tags, uppercase
eyebrows, icon-in-a-tile feature grids, the cream + thin serif + terracotta
"editorial AI" look.

### Design Principles
1. One idea per screen, one primary action.
2. Structure with type, space and rules before boxes.
3. Numbers are content: large, exact, never decorative.
4. Colour carries meaning: green go / High School, copper IELTS, yellow
   attention, red wrong.
5. Every state is designed: empty, loading, error, done, offline.
